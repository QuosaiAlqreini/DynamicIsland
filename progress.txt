- **Learnings for future iterations:**
  - In the LL hook, swallow the final trigger/end key (return 1) and retype it, instead of racing the app for the keystroke.
  - Always set argtypes/restype for CallNextHookEx/SetWindowsHookExW on 64-bit Python or LPARAM gets truncated.
  - Ignore events with LLKHF_INJECTED so our own SendInput output doesn't re-trigger hotstrings.
  - Wait for Ctrl/Alt/Win to be released before SendInput from a hotkey, otherwise typed text becomes shortcuts.
  - Escape '&' as '&&' in QPushButton text (Qt mnemonic).
